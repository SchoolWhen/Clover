# Clover Roadmap

The roadmap is organized around research questions rather than version numbers. It is intentionally evidence-driven and may change when a more fundamental contradiction is found.

## Phase 0 — Public Research Foundation

**Current**

- define the research question and product goal;
- establish public research principles;
- separate current facts, hypotheses and history;
- build the research map and evaluation map;
- document open questions and terminology;
- initialize the public GitHub repository.

## Phase 1 — Understanding the Problem

- classical computer vision and geometry;
- self-supervised visual/video representation;
- spatial intelligence and learned geometry;
- predictive and generative world models;
- embodied reasoning and VLA;
- robot foundation models;
- active perception, model-based robotics and lifelong learning.

**Exit condition:** we can explain what leading approaches represent, what they demonstrably achieve, and what remains unresolved for the Clover question.

## Phase 2 — Representation Research

Compare candidate answers to the central representation question:

- explicit geometry/state;
- distributed latent representation;
- object-centric representation;
- fields and machine-native primitives;
- spatiotemporal structure;
- hybrid representations.

## Phase 3 — World Formation

Study how a representation is acquired and maintained from visual experience:

- self-supervision;
- prediction and reconstruction;
- temporal persistence;
- physical reasoning;
- memory and belief revision;
- causal and counterfactual structure;
- active perception and interaction.

## Phase 4 — Robot Brain

Connect world understanding to:

- Decision;
- Tool Use;
- Execution;
- replanning and recovery.

The key question is whether a persistent world representation produces measurable embodied value over direct action models.

## Phase 5 — Evaluation

Use public benchmarks and real tasks as instruments:

- world/physical understanding;
- causality and counterfactuals;
- open-world generalization;
- long-horizon embodied tasks;
- baseline comparison and ablation;
- real-world transfer.

## Phase 6 — Hardware Co-design

Map the validated computation to the most appropriate substrate:

- CPU;
- GPU;
- Metal / Apple Silicon unified memory;
- other accelerators where justified;
- latency, memory and energy optimization.

Hardware is optimized around the scientific mechanism, not used to choose the mechanism in advance.
