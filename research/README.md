# Research

Clover is currently in the **Survey / Research Foundation** phase.

The primary entry point is:

> **[Clover Survey](survey/README.md)**

The survey is intentionally flat and content-heavy. It synthesizes prior work, frontier methods, primary papers, benchmarks/tasks, datasets/code, research questions and active Clover hypotheses around the fixed SSOT:

> **How can a machine understand the real world through vision alone?**

## Current research surfaces

- [`survey/README.md`](survey/README.md) — start here.
- [`survey/FRONTIER_METHODS.md`](survey/FRONTIER_METHODS.md) — detailed method families and strongest current answers.
- [`survey/PAPERS.md`](survey/PAPERS.md) — curated papers, project pages and code links.
- [`survey/BENCHMARKS_TASKS.md`](survey/BENCHMARKS_TASKS.md) — diagnostic benchmarks and embodied task hierarchy.
- [`survey/DATASETS_CODE.md`](survey/DATASETS_CODE.md) — datasets, repositories and implementation resources.
- [`survey/RESEARCH_QUESTIONS.md`](survey/RESEARCH_QUESTIONS.md) — cross-cutting technical questions that must be answered before the next architecture is frozen.
- [`survey/CLOVER_HYPOTHESES.md`](survey/CLOVER_HYPOTHESES.md) — screened hypotheses retained from earlier Clover work.
- [`survey/FRONTIER_SIGNALS.md`](survey/FRONTIER_SIGNALS.md) — filtered 2026 signals that are worth tracking but are not automatically project truth.

## Supporting material

- `literature/` — source indices and narrower literature notes.
- `decisions/` — research decision records (ADR style).
- `../docs/research-map/` — earlier map-style organization, retained as secondary reference.
- `../docs/research-questions/` — earlier question-by-question synthesis, retained as secondary reference.

## Rule

Raw news, a strong paper, or a company demo does not become a Clover mechanism by appearing here. Evidence must first be understood, classified and related to the central research contradiction.
