# Literature

This directory is the curated prior-art layer for Clover.

The goal is not to collect every paper. It is to understand the strongest competing answers to:

> **How can a machine understand the real world through vision alone?**

## Files

- [Model & Literature Index](model-index.md) — navigation across representative systems and papers.
- [Datasets & Benchmarks](datasets-and-benchmarks.md) — visual, robot and evaluation resources relevant to the question.

## Reading template

For each important work, record:

1. research problem;
2. representation;
3. learning objective;
4. data;
5. treatment of space;
6. treatment of time;
7. memory;
8. physical assumptions;
9. uncertainty/observability;
10. action interface;
11. demonstrated results;
12. known limitations;
13. evidence type;
14. contradiction or opportunity for Clover.

## Policy

A paper does not become a Clover mechanism merely because it is strong. Prior work enters Clover only through understanding the contradiction it solves. Integration by framework stacking is explicitly discouraged.
