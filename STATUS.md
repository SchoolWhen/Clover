# Clover Status

Last updated: 2026-10-14

## Research Question

> **How can a machine understand the real world through vision alone?**

## Product Goal

> Build a general-purpose robotic brain that forms an understanding of the real world through vision alone, then uses that understanding to decide and act.

## Current Phase

**Phase 0 — Public Research Foundation: substantially established.**  
**Phase 1 — Understanding the Problem: active.**

The immediate work is systematic prior-art and research-map development, not benchmark optimization or large-scale reproduction.

## Public Repository — IMPLEMENTED

The repository now includes:

- research SSOT and product goal;
- research principles;
- roadmap and status vocabulary;
- detailed research map across classical vision, learned representations, video world models, spatial intelligence, object-centric representation, embodied reasoning, VLA, robot foundation models, model-based robotics, active perception, causality and lifelong learning;
- model/literature and dataset/benchmark indices;
- evaluation principles and working Understanding Battery;
- active research hypotheses;
- research decision records and contribution templates.

## Existing Clover Prototype — RESEARCH_PROTOTYPE

A substantial private Research Prototype 1 exists from the pre-reset phase, exploring:

- field-based visual evidence and crystallization;
- persistent entities/spacetime support;
- typed world queries;
- epistemic licence infrastructure;
- decision, tool-use and execution loops.

It is **not yet imported into this new public repository**. Import will require a dedicated audit for private paths, secrets, historical SSOT drift, build state and scientific overclaims.

## Active Research Questions — OPEN

High-priority questions include:

- explicit vs distributed world representation;
- prediction vs reconstruction vs generation;
- object primitive vs emergent structure;
- geometry vs learned representation;
- passive observation vs interaction;
- role of language priors in apparent physical understanding;
- persistent memory and belief revision;
- active perception;
- causal/intervention learning;
- measurable value of a worldview for robot action.

## Research Hypotheses — RESEARCH_PROTOTYPE / OPEN

Tracked hypotheses include:

- field crystallization;
- machine-native primitives / CSP-like representation;
- explicit spacetime world state;
- epistemic licences;
- world growth and revision;
- explicit-vs-latent hybrid representations.

None is currently a frozen architectural commitment.

## Evaluation — DESIGNING

We have catalogued relevant instruments including IntPhys 2, MVPBench, CausalVQA, Perception Test, Ego4D, LIBERO, CALVIN and RoboCasa365. A working multi-axis Understanding Battery is documented, but the current phase is **not** running a full benchmark campaign.

## Not Yet Demonstrated

Clover has not yet publicly demonstrated:

- general real-world visual world understanding;
- comprehensive positive-result SOTA;
- robust open-world generalization;
- persistent long-horizon real-robot world state;
- general causal understanding;
- long-horizon autonomous robot-brain capability;
- an empirically established final representation;
- a final CPU/GPU/Metal hardware allocation.

These remain targets, not reasons to lower the research question.
