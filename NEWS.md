# Clover News — Archive Index

The project's continuous frontier watch, kept **flat at the repository root**: every edition is one root-level file, no subfolders.

## Naming

```text
news_yymmddhhmm.md
```

`yy` year · `mm` month · `dd` day · `hh` hour · `mm` minute (Asia/Shanghai), taken from the moment the edition is created. Each edition is a new file; editions are never edited after publication except to append a dated correction note.

## What each edition collects

1. **Morning digests** — daily embodied-AI / AGI / LLM / robotics briefings (30 items: 15 frontier papers & methods + 15 industry items + implications for this project).
2. **Evening digests** — daily Machine Native deep briefings (30 items + implications + daily open questions).
3. **Frontier design-tracking reports** — competitor and method-optimization tracking: what changed, and how our method should update in response.

## Evidence discipline

This archive is a **discovery / signal archive** (`SURVEY_SIGNAL` in the labels of `AGENTS.md` §4.5) and, per `AGENTS.md` §2, a **staging inbox**: it is not SSOT, it is not scientific evidence, and no signal may terminate here. An item becomes usable evidence only after it is upgraded to a primary paper, official project page or official reported result and recorded in the owning direction document under `01-research/`, its source entry in `01-research/sources-and-resources.md`, and the structured record in `registry/papers.jsonl`.
