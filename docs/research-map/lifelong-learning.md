# Lifelong Learning and World Growth

## Core problem

A general-purpose robot cannot require complete retraining whenever it encounters a new object, relationship or physical regularity.

Desired capabilities include:

- online belief revision;
- memory consolidation;
- learning new types/regularities;
- retaining important old knowledge;
- forgetting irrelevant detail without destroying provenance;
- adapting without catastrophic forgetting.

## Representative benchmark direction

LIBERO is useful as a lifelong robot-learning instrument:

## Clover questions

- Which parts of world knowledge should update online?
- Can ontology/type growth happen without corrupting older world state?
- What is the relationship between persistent episodic evidence and abstract world knowledge?
