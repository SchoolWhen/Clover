# Robot Foundation Models

## Why this area matters

The strongest robot foundation systems are the final capability reference for Clover: they show what real robots can already do in unfamiliar physical environments.

## Figure Helix 2.5

Figure reports zero-shot whole-body long-horizon behavior across unseen real homes, including tidying, towel folding and bed making.

- https://www.figure.ai/news/helix-2-5-zero-shot-30-home-generalization

The research question for Clover is not whether to copy Helix, but what internal world competence is necessary to reproduce or exceed this level of generalization and recovery.

## Skild S1

Skild presents a general-purpose robotic brain and in-context task specification through demonstrations.

- https://www.skild.ai/blogs/s1
- https://www.skild.ai/blogs/building-the-general-purpose-robotic-brain

Physical self-play is also relevant to the question of how interaction creates knowledge unavailable in passive video:

- https://skild.ai/blogs/physical-self-play

## Clover questions

- How much capability comes from scale and data rather than representation structure?
- What does explicit persistent world state buy once a policy is already highly general?
- Which tasks expose failures that cannot be hidden by behavioral success rates alone?
