# Causality and Counterfactual Reasoning

## What problem does this area solve?

Prediction answers what tends to happen. Causal understanding asks what changes under intervention and why.

## Research concepts

- intervention;
- counterfactuals;
- action-conditioned prediction;
- causal representation learning;
- event structure and dependency discovery.

## Diagnostic benchmark

CausalVQA is tracked as a video-based causal/counterfactual reasoning instrument:

- https://ai.meta.com/research/publications/causalvqa-a-physically-grounded-causal-reasoning-benchmark-for-video-models/

## Clover questions

- Can causality be learned from passive visual observation alone?
- What additional information is introduced by robot interaction?
- Can contradiction/counter-witness events reveal a minimal affected world dependency?
- How should causal hypotheses differ from licensed world facts?
