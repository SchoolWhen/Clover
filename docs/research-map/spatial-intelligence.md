# Spatial Intelligence

## What problem does this area solve?

Recover, represent or generate coherent spatial worlds from visual observations.

## Representative work

### VGGT

A learned feed-forward geometry model estimating cameras, depth, point maps and tracks.

- Code: https://github.com/facebookresearch/vggt

### VGGT-Ω

Extends scalable spatial reconstruction and dynamic-scene reasoning.

- Project: https://vggt-omega.github.io/
- Code: https://github.com/facebookresearch/vggt-omega

### DUSt3R

Learned multi-view reconstruction through point-map style representations.

- Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.14132

### World Labs Atlas

A general spatial/world-model direction connecting reconstruction, generation and simulation.

- https://www.worldlabs.ai/blog/atlas

## Why it matters to Clover

A major open question is whether reconstruction quality is merely a useful perceptual capability or a scalable proxy for world understanding. Clover must distinguish geometry, spatial memory, physical dynamics and task-level abstraction rather than assuming they are the same problem.
