# Clover Research Principles

These principles govern research and engineering decisions in Clover.

## 1. The question comes first

The highest SSOT is:

> **How can a machine understand the real world through vision alone?**

Architecture, benchmark, hardware and historical implementation are subordinate to the question.

## 2. Evidence before commitment

Study the strongest existing explanations before freezing a mechanism. A Clover idea can be rewritten or removed when evidence changes.

## 3. Benchmarks are instruments

A benchmark diagnoses a capability. It is not the research objective and cannot by itself establish world understanding.

## 4. Real capability matters

The eventual result must matter for embodied behavior in unfamiliar physical environments, not only for offline classification or QA.

## 5. Separate facts from hypotheses

Every important statement should be identifiable as implemented, tested, scientifically validated, hypothesized, planned, open or superseded.

## 6. No framework stacking

External work is not imported as a pile of modules. We first ask what contradiction it solves, whether Clover has the same contradiction, and whether a Clover-native formulation is justified.

## 7. Hardware is a design variable

CPU, GPU, Metal and other accelerators are selected according to the computation. Hardware must not predetermine the scientific answer.

## 8. Important mechanisms must be falsifiable

A research mechanism needs a discriminatory experiment and a kill condition. A beautiful name or analogy is not evidence.

## 9. Code must remain inspectable

Avoid unnecessary layers, duplicated authority, hidden state and complexity that makes scientific behavior difficult to audit.

## 10. Understanding must eventually matter for action

A world representation must eventually demonstrate measurable value for decision, planning, recovery or action. Otherwise its status as a useful robotic world model remains open.

## 11. Preserve research history

Superseded ideas are archived, not rewritten into the current story. Research decisions should remain traceable.

## 12. The answer remains open

Clover may ultimately use explicit state, distributed latent representations, geometry, learned dynamics, fields, object-centric structure, hybrids, or something not yet identified. The project exists to find out.
